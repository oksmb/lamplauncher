LampLauncher cams

Add and edit the cams on the TV's globe. The default cam (★) opens in the YouTube app when the TV turns on; set it here, or on the TV by holding OK on a cam.

One more setup step: LampLauncher isn't the box's home app yet, so pressing power in the morning may bring back Google TV's home screen instead of your default cam. From a computer with adb connected to the box, run: 

    See Claude-README.md for details and how to undo it.
